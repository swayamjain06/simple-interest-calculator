# Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate the interest on a starting amount and the total after a given time. Simple interest is calculated on the original principal throughout the term.

## Formula

**Simple interest = P × R × T ÷ 100**

| Input | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| P | Principal, the starting amount in your chosen currency |
| R | Annual interest rate as a percentage. Enter 5 for 5%. |
| T | Time in years. For six months, enter 0.5. |

**Total amount = principal + simple interest**

## Example

With a principal of 10,000, an annual rate of 5%, and a term of 2 years, the simple interest is 10,000 × 5 × 2 ÷ 100 = 1,000. The total amount is 11,000.

## Usage

1. Download [index.html](index.html) and open it in a web browser.
2. Enter the principal, annual interest rate, and time in years. All three inputs accept nonnegative decimals.
3. Select **Calculate** to see the interest and total, each displayed to two decimal places in the same currency as the principal.
